Voter Page Header Text

The two lines shown on the voter page, just below the icons and above "Vote for the next song." Either can be left blank — neither has to be a show name or a date; put whatever announcement you want there.

Settings

Categories

The chips voters see for this show. Order here is chip order. Removing a category that songs still carry does not touch those songs — it just stops rendering a chip for them, so check the warning below before saving.

Reconcile with FPP

Reads the live playlist from FPP and syncs songs in or out. Additive and idempotent — never overwrites a category a human already set, and a song that leaves the playlist is deactivated, not deleted.
